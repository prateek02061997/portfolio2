Kiwi Budget Tours

Website case study focused on conversion, customer planning experience, and AI-assisted journey support.

Overview

A tourism platform designed to make itinerary discovery and booking guidance faster and more customer-friendly.

Business Goal

Increase direct inquiry quality and conversion by reducing trip-planning friction for first-time visitors.

Client Requirements

Simple navigation, clear package information, trust signals, and support for guided itinerary decisions.

Solution

Built a conversion-focused site architecture with strong service pages, guided content flow, and clear CTA placement.

Features

AI-assisted planning concept, structured package presentation, inquiry-first UX, and mobile-first layout responsiveness.

Technology Stack

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, AI workflow concepts, analytics-driven iteration.
